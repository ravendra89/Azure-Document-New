# So, our Question should be: Why do we need monitoring for any type of cloud services or resources?

Answer is:
Using cloud services or resources alone is not enough. We also need to monitor their utilization, performance, and availability to ensure they are operating
efficiently and reliably. Monitoring helps us to identify issues such as unexpected downtimes, performance bottlenecks, or resource overutilization before they impact users.
It also helps with managing costs, ensuring security, and meeting compliance requirements.. That’s why monitoring to cloud services is essential.
